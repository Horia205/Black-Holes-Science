FUN FACTS ABOUT BLACK HOLES

  1. Black holes can "burp" energy

    2. As matter falls toward a black hole, some of it gets blasted back into space as powerful jets traveling near the speed of light.

  2. A black hole can wander through space

    3. Not all black holes stay in one place. Some may be ejected from their galaxies after collisions with other black holes.

  3. Black holes can create sound waves

    4. In 2003, astronomers detected pressure waves from a black hole in the Perseus galaxy cluster. The "sound" is much too low for humans to hear.

  4. The biggest black holes are larger than our entire solar system

    5. Some supermassive black holes have event horizons so large they could extend beyond the orbit of Neptune.

  5. Two black holes can orbit each other like dancers

    6. Before merging, they spiral around one another millions of times and release enormous amounts of energy as gravitational waves.
