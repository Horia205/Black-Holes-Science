The Science of Black Holes

Be ready to discover the amazing world of black holes.

Visual of a black hole:

Interesting facts:

  • Black holes can slow down time
  • They aren't actually cosmic vacuum cleaners
  • You can't see a black hole directly
  • Black holes can spin incredibly fast
  • Black holes can evaporate over time
  • A black hole's "point of no return" is called the event horizon

Types of Black Holes

  1. Stellar Black Holes: Formed when massive stars collapse after running out of fuel.
  2. Intermediate Black Holes: Larger than stellar black holes but smaller than supermassive ones.
  3. Supermassive Black Holes: Found at the centers of most galaxies, including the Milky Way.
  4. Primordial Black Holes: Hypothetical black holes that may have formed shortly after the Big Bang.
